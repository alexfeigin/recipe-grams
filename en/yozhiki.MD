# Yozhiki

_Stuffed Cabbage Filling as Meatballs_

## Ingredients:

- 1 kg ground beef
- 200g dry jasmine rice
- 700g onion, diced
- 200g carrot, shredded
- 240g tomato concentrate (22 Brix)
- 200g ice cubes
- Salt, ground black pepper, granulated garlic — to taste
- Water (for cooking liquid)

## Instructions:

1. Sauté the onion and carrot until glassy, just starting to brown. Salt as you go.
2. Sauté the tomato concentrate separately until it starts to darken.
3. Mix the tomato into the onion-carrot.
4. Add the ice cubes to cool the mix.
5. Reserve 25% of the mix and return it to the sauté pan. Add water to at least the same depth as the reserved mix. Salt the water to taste.
6. To the remaining 75%, add the beef, season with salt, pepper, and garlic, then add the rice.
7. Microwave a tablespoon-size scoop of the meat mix until done. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and garlic as needed.
8. Form balls and drop them into the simmering liquid.
9. Simmer for about 30 minutes and it's done.
