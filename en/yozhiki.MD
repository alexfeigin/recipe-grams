# Yozhiki

_Stuffed Cabbage Filling as Meatballs Because We're Lazy_

## Ingredients

- 1 kg ground beef
- 200 g dry jasmine rice
- 700 g onion, diced
- 200 g carrot, shredded
- 240 g tomato concentrate (22° Brix)
- 200 g ice cubes
- Salt, ground black pepper, and granulated garlic — to taste
- Water (for the cooking liquid)

## Instructions

1. Everything is done using one large sauté pan (or rondeau) and one large mixing bowl for the meat mixture.
2. Sauté the onion and carrot until translucent and just beginning to brown. Season with salt as you go.
3. Transfer the onion and carrot to the mixing bowl.
4. In the same sauté pan (no need to wash it), sauté the tomato concentrate until it begins to darken.
5. Mix the tomato concentrate into the onion and carrot in the bowl.
6. Add the ice cubes to cool the mixture.
7. Reserve about 25% of the mixture and return it to the sauté pan. Add enough water so the liquid reaches about one-quarter of the height of the pan. Season the liquid with salt to taste.
8. Add the ground beef to the remaining 75% of the mixture and season with salt, black pepper, and granulated garlic.
9. Microwave a small meatball (about one tablespoon of the mixture) until cooked through. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
10. Add the rice, mix well, form meatballs, and gently place them into the simmering liquid.
11. Simmer gently for about 30 minutes, until the meatballs are cooked through and the rice is tender.
