# Super-Easy Banana Cake

The batter is slightly too much for one loaf pan. Fill the pan almost to the top and bake the remainder in about 3 cupcake wells, or bake the entire batch as cupcakes.

## Ingredients

- 330g very ripe bananas
- 100g eggs, about 2 eggs without shells
- 70g oil
- 100g brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 210g self-rising flour
- 2-3g baking soda
- 1g salt
- 100g chopped dark chocolate or chocolate chips
- About 30g chopped dark chocolate or chocolate chips for topping, optional

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 170-180°C (340-355°F).
2. Place the bananas, eggs, oil, brown sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla in a bowl. Blend with an immersion blender or a regular blender until smooth.
3. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Mix only until the batter is uniform.
4. Fold in 100g chopped chocolate or chocolate chips until evenly distributed.
5. Transfer to a loaf pan or cupcake tin. Sprinkle with the remaining chocolate, if using.
6. Bake for 30-40 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
