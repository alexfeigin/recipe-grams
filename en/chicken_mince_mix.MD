# Chicken Mince Mixture

A chicken mince mixture for [oven-baked chicken meatballs](./chicken_meatballs.MD) or as a filling for [pelmeni dough](./pilmeni_dough.MD).

## Ingredients

- 1kg boneless chicken thigh mince (or 500g thigh mince and 500g breast mince for a leaner option)
- 750g finely chopped onion (weight before cooking; 75% of the meat weight)
- About 100g ice cubes
- A little olive oil, if sweating the onions in a pan
- Water, as needed while sweating the onions in a pan
- 13g salt
- Black pepper, granulated garlic, and cayenne pepper, to taste (also works with [grill rub](./grill_rub.MD))

Thigh mince gives a richer, softer texture. Breast mince contains very little fat and makes a leaner mixture.

## Instructions

1. Precook the onions using either method:
   - **Pan:** Sweat the onions with a little olive oil until softened. Add a little water periodically to prevent browning or burning while the onions cook evenly.
   - **Microwave:** Place the chopped onions in a microwave-safe dish and microwave for 4–5 minutes, until softened.
2. Transfer the hot onions to a mixing bowl and immediately add about 100g ice cubes. Stir until the ice melts, then let the onions cool. Keep all the liquid for the mixture.
3. Mix the cooled onions and their liquid with the chicken mince, salt, and spices by hand (gloves recommended).

## Uses

- **Meatballs:** Shape and bake according to the [oven-baked chicken meatballs recipe](./chicken_meatballs.MD). Makes about 1 oven tray.
- **Pelmeni filling:** Use with the [pelmeni dough recipe](./pilmeni_dough.MD). Adjust the filling quantity to the amount of dough.
- **Leftover filling:** Shape any leftover pelmeni filling into meatballs and cook in the oven or air fryer.
