# Goldilocks and the Three Bears Style Cookies

## Ingredients

- 140 g flour
- 100 g cold butter, cubed
- 2 tablespoons Shokolit baking chocolate powder
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- 60 g powdered sugar
- A pinch of salt
- 1 egg yolk
- A very small amount of water or milk, only if needed to bring the dough together

## Instructions

1. Place the flour, butter, Shokolit, powdered sugar, salt, and baking powder in a food processor. Process until crumbly.
2. Add the egg yolk and process just until clumps of dough form. Even if the mixture still looks powdery, try pressing it together by hand. If it does not hold together, add a very small amount of water or milk. The dough should feel soft and pliable.
3. Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
4. Preheat the oven to 170°C and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
5. Roll the dough into a thin sheet on a floured surface and cut out shapes using your favorite cookie cutters.
6. Place on the lined tray and bake for just 8–10 minutes. The cookies will be very soft when they come out of the oven and will firm up as they cool. Let them cool before transferring to a container.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container at room temperature.
