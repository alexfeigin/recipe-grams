# Easy Sweet Carrot Muffins or Loaf Cake

**16 muffins or 1 loaf cake | 4 servings**

## Ingredients

- 300g carrots, finely grated
- 6 eggs
- 113g sweetened applesauce
- 25g maple syrup
- 20g oil
- 5g vanilla extract
- 120g white self-rising flour
- 1–2g ground cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Finely grate the carrots and transfer them to a microwave-safe bowl. Cover loosely and microwave for 2–3 minutes. Stir and check: the carrots should be slightly soft and flexible, but not mashed. If they are still firm, microwave for 1 more minute. There is no need to add water or squeeze out the carrots.
2. Let the carrots cool slightly while preheating the oven to 175°C (350°F).
3. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, applesauce, maple syrup, oil, and vanilla until smooth.
4. Add the softened carrots and mix.
5. Add the self-rising flour, cinnamon, and salt. Mix gently just until the flour is incorporated; do not overmix.
6. Divide the batter among 16 wells of a muffin tin, or transfer it to a loaf pan lined with parchment paper.
7. Bake according to the pan:
   - Muffins: about 22–27 minutes.
   - Loaf cake: about 40–50 minutes; begin checking after 35 minutes.
8. The muffins or cake are ready when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out without wet batter and with a few moist crumbs.
9. Cool for at least 10–15 minutes before removing from the pan or slicing.
